Junseong Shin

Ph.D. Student · Hanyang University, VILAB

I am a Ph.D. student at VILAB, advised by Prof. Tae Hyun Kim. I build generative priors — diffusion and flow matching — to solve inverse problems in vision. My work spans image restoration (reflection removal, dehazing, shadow removal) and is expanding toward video generation and 3D scene understanding.

I received my B.S. in Mathematics from Hanyang University, and I now leverage this background in Bayesian methods and generative models for vision.

I am on the job market for research scientist and postdoctoral positions starting in 2027.
